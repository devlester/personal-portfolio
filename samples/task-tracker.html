How it helps a client

  • No surprises

    Overdue tasks turn red and tasks due within two days turn amber, so problems show up before the deadline, not after.

  • Clear blockers

    “Waiting on client” separates what I’m working on from what needs your answer or file.

  • Easy updates

    The Summary tab counts tasks by status and priority and shows % complete, ready to paste into an end-of-day report.
