How to list a new product
on Shopify.

A standard operating procedure written so anyone on the team can add a product the same way, every time. This is the kind of SOP I write when I take over a recurring task, so the process stays consistent even when someone else does it.

Purpose & scope

This SOP explains how to add a new product to the Northbay Supply Co. Shopify store so that every listing has complete information, consistent naming, and correct stock. It covers single products and products with variants. It does not cover bundles or gift cards.

Owner: E-commerce VA · Approver: Store manager · Review: every 6 months, or when Shopify changes the product page

Procedure

STEP 01Gather the product details

  • Open the product info sheet from the client (or supplier) and confirm you have: product name, description notes, price, compare-at price (if on sale), cost, SKU, barcode, weight, sizes/colors, and photos.
  • If anything is missing, list it and ask the client once, in one message, before you start.

STEP 02Prepare the photos

  • Use square images, at least 2048 × 2048 px, on a consistent background.
  • Rename files before uploading: sku-01.jpg in lowercase (for example nb-wkd-olv-01.jpg). Clear names help search engines and make files easy to find later.

STEP 03Create the product

  • In Shopify admin, go to Products → Add product.
  • Set Status to Draft straight away, so nothing goes live before it is checked.

STEP 04Write the title and description

  • Title format: Product name – Key feature – Color. Example: Canvas Weekender Bag – Waxed Cotton – Olive.
  • Description: a 1–2 sentence intro, 3–5 short bullet points of features, then materials, size, and care instructions. Use the client’s brand voice guide if there is one.

STEP 05Add media

  • Upload photos in order: main product image first, then angles, details, and lifestyle shots.
  • Add alt text to every image describing what it shows (for example “Olive canvas weekender bag, front view”).

STEP 06Organize the product

  • Fill in Product category, Product type, and Vendor.
  • Add the product to the right Collections and add agreed Tags (see the table below).

STEP 07Set pricing

  • Enter Price. Enter Compare-at price only if the product is on sale.
  • Enter Cost per item so the client can see margins. Leave Charge tax on unless told otherwise.

STEP 08Set inventory and shipping

  • Enter SKU and Barcode exactly as in the product sheet. Turn on Track quantity and enter stock per location.
  • Under Shipping, tick “This is a physical product” and enter the packed weight.

STEP 09Add variants

  • Add options (for example Color, Size). Shopify creates one variant per combination.
  • Open each variant and check its SKU, price, quantity, and image. Delete combinations that don’t exist.

STEP 10Edit the search engine listing

  • Page title: up to 70 characters, include the main keyword.
  • Meta description: up to 160 characters, written to make someone click. Check the URL handle is short and readable.

STEP 11Review and publish

  • Click Preview and check the page on desktop and mobile.
  • Run the quality checklist below. When everything passes, set Status to Active and confirm the correct Sales channels.

STEP 12Log it

  • Update the task tracker: mark the task Done, add the product link, and note anything the client needs to decide.

Naming rules

Field Rule Example
SKU BRAND-PRODUCT-COLOR(-SIZE), capitals, dashes only NB-WKD-OLV
Image file sku-01.jpg, lowercase nb-wkd-olv-01.jpg
Tags Lowercase, from the approved tag list only bags, travel, new-arrival
URL handle Product name, lowercase, dashes, no color canvas-weekender-bag

Quality checklist

  • Title follows the format and has no typos
  • Every image has alt text, and the main image is first
  • Price, compare-at price, and cost match the product sheet
  • Every variant has its own SKU, price, stock, and image
  • Weight is entered, so shipping rates calculate correctly
  • Product is in the right collections and has approved tags only
  • SEO title and description are filled in and within length
  • Page checked on mobile before setting it to Active

Common mistakes

  • Publishing too early. Always start in Draft and switch to Active only after the checklist passes.
  • Copy-paste SKUs between variants. Duplicate SKUs break stock tracking and reports.
  • Compare-at price with no sale. It shows a fake discount to customers. Leave it empty unless there is a real sale.
  • Forgetting stock per location. The product shows as sold out even though it is on the shelf.
