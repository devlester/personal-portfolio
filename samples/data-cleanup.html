What I fixed, and how

Every fix is a formula on its own column, so the work is easy to check and repeat on next month’s export.

  • Names

    TRIM removes extra spaces, PROPER fixes capitals: “  maria SANTOS ” → “Maria Santos”.

  • Emails

    TRIM + LOWER, so the same address always matches, whatever the capitals.

  • Phone numbers

    Strip spaces, dashes, brackets and +, convert 05… and 00971… to 971…, then format as +971 5X XXX XXXX.

  • Cities

    A small lookup table maps “dubai”, “DXB”, “AUH”, “SHJ” and others to one standard name with INDEX/MATCH.

  • Totals

    Remove “AED” and commas, then convert the text to a real number, so totals and sorting work.

  • Duplicates

    COUNTIF on the cleaned email flags the second appearance of a customer. Removed: 2 rows.

Why it matters

Clean data means the customer count is right, the sales total is right, and the list can go straight into a CRM, an email tool, or a delivery sheet without someone fixing it by hand. Because the steps are formulas, the same cleanup takes minutes next time.
